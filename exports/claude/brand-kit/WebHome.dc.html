Questions

What comes first on raw land?−

In Tangipahoa it’s your 911 address, then the culvert permit, then us: clear, driveway, pad. The Dirt Order walks it step by step.

Do you do the whole thing or just clearing?+

Either. Most folks want the whole dirt side: we clear it, cut in the driveway and build your pad, in that order. We can do one step if that’s all you need.

Do you set the pad height?+

No. Your surveyor and the parish set the height. We build to it, and we’ll show you where to look it up.

How far do you travel?+

Hammond, Tangipahoa Parish & the Northshore. Call or text and ask about your spot. (Full radius: W-07.)

Soil Black, Surface, Elevated · Bone and Bone Alt · Earth Brown and Clay · Cattail Rust and Copper (5%). With eyes half shut, a page reads as dark earth with bone type and one rust button.

Colour rules
  • One thing in rust. The primary button is the rust thing on a screen. Copper numerals and one rust rule may sit with it; a second solid rust fill may not.
  • Never add green, safety yellow, royal blue or any cool accent as a brand colour. Status green is for form success only.
  • No gradients, rust to copper included. No shadows, no glows. Flat fills only.
  • Copper is for dark grounds. On Bone it is a focus ring or a numeral of 24 px or larger, never body text or a link.
  • Colour is never the only signal: links are underlined, errors have text and an icon, the current nav item is underlined.
  • No black, champagne, gold or hi-vis yellow fields. No photo tint except the Soil overlay at 60 to 75%.

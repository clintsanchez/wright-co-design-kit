PhotoSlot. Soil Surface at the final crop ratio, 8 px radius (square when full bleed), Clay hairline, a filled Clay camera, a label that always begins “Real job photo:”. It stays visibly a slot and never ships in a public piece: a page or post with no photo is type-led instead. BeforeAfterSlot: two slots divided by a 4 px rust rule, caption bar beneath.

Shot list for the owner
  1. The hero series: woods, cleared, driveway in, pad built, all from one marked spot
  2. A driveway cut, with the culvert at the road
  3. A finished pad, with the builder’s stakes
  4. A pond holding water
  5. Wright Co.’s own machines working the ground
  6. The crew working; Cody walking a lot, with permission
  7. Vertical video, 10 to 30 seconds, from the fixed spot

For each: town, date, the work, who shot it, permission. Originals, not screenshots.

When photos arrive
  1. Real Louisiana Wright Co. jobs only, logged in the manifest
  2. Show the ground, not the gear; crop out other companies’ equipment
  3. One grade: warm, slightly desaturated, greens down 15–20%, clay kept true
  4. Crop and straighten only. No compositing, fill or upscaling
  5. Ratios 4:5, 1:1, 16:9 only
  6. Caption town and work: “Independence · 3 ac cleared · 300 ft drive · pad” is a format example only
  7. Text on a photo only under a 60–75% Soil overlay
  8. A landowner asks, it comes down the same day
AI image framework: not used. Wright Co. wins by showing real lots; a generated lot would be a fabricated job. No generated lots, machines, people, places, logos or mock-ups. Use the shot list and a labelled photo slot. Photos from the owner’s other business are never used.

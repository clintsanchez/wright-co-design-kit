Headlines and buttons

Plain statements that name the buyer’s moment, the step or the real question. Six to eight words, two lines at most in Graduate: “You bought the land. We’ll get it ready to build.” · “From the first tree to the house pad.” · “Just closed on land? Here’s what happens first.” · “One crew. Your land. In order.” · “What moves the price”. Two buttons only: Book a Free Lot Walk and Call or Text 360-957-5865. Text links: “Ask about the Homeplace Package” · “Read The Dirt Order” · “See what moves the price”.

Tone by situation
WebsitePlainest and most confident. Outcome, then the sequence, then one call to action
Sales, estimatesMost serious. Line items, the sequence, no humour, no adjectives
Texts and callsCody, first person, warm, one question per text, under 300 characters
SocialLooser, a little grit, dry humour; never about money, water or the customer
The Dirt OrderPatient and complete. The answer in the first sentence. Explains, never certifies
Weather or delayCalm, early, specific: what changed and when we’re back. No excuses, no guarantees

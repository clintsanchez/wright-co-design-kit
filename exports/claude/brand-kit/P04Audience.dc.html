Position
Positioning statement (internal, never a headline). For people around Hammond who’ve bought raw land to build a home, Wright Co. is the home-site prep crew that takes your lot from woods to build-ready (cleared, driveway in, house pad built) with one crew, because we do the dirt side and only the dirt side, in order, with no hand-offs. In four words: build-ready, one crew, in order.

Core message. Wright Co. helps people around Hammond who’ve bought land to build a home get it build-ready, by sending one crew to clear it, cut in the driveway and build the house pad, in order, with no hand-offs. Every headline traces back to its four parts: the finish line, the sequence, one crew and the place.

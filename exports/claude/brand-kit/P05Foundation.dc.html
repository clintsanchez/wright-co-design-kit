The visual idea. The page should look like the ground itself, and like a local crew’s truck door rather than a franchise website: dark soil, rust and bone, heavy collegiate lettering, square edges.

Purpose

Wright Co. exists so that someone who just bought raw land to build a home doesn’t have to become a general contractor to get the dirt side done.

Mission

Take raw Louisiana land and make it build-ready, with one crew from the first tree to the finished house pad: a walk of the land, a plain-language plan, the right order, a clean site handed to the builder.

Vision

Be the crew people around Hammond name first when someone says “I bought land, now what?”

Personality

Rugged. Straight-talking. Dependable. Louisiana-proud. Hardworking. Everyman in the voice, Hero in the work. Louisiana-proud is carried by the emblem, never claimed in words.

Values, in the order of the work
  1. Start to finish, build-ready. We own the whole dirt sequence, so there’s no gap between subs for a problem to fall into.
  2. Straight answers. You hear what drives the cost before you hear a number. When it’s the surveyor’s or the parish’s call, we say so.
  3. Show up. Returning a call is part of the job. The callback standard is W-10
  4. Know the ground. South Louisiana land is wet and clay-heavy. We learn each lot before we quote it.
  5. Leave it right. The job’s done when the site is clean and the builder can start.
Four pillars
  1. One crew does the whole dirt side.
  2. Done in the right order, and we’ll show you the order. 01 Clear · 02 Driveway · 03 Pad · 04 Foundation Dig.
  3. Straight answers before a number.
  4. Real lots, start to finish. No proof yet. Runs only as: “We’re new to the Hammond area, so we’re showing our work one lot at a time.”
Brand promise, held: “Written scope. Firm price. We call you back.” Only with the badge and a change-order rule (W-09, W-10). The safe line, usable now: “Straight answers, in plain words. Call or text Cody.”
Tagline and descriptors

Short form: Ready to build.
Placements: From the first tree to the house pad.
Builders only: One crew. Woods to build-ready.

Held, off by default: “Ready to build. The Wright way.” W-21
Retired, on nothing: “Built on Hard Work. Driven by Quality.” · “Work Done the Wright Way”.
Refuses to be: badges and superlatives, a general contractor, a per-acre mulching shop, borrowed proof, a competitor-knocker.

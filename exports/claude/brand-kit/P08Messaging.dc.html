Objection handling
How long have you been doing this?
“I’ve been clearing and excavating since 2022.”
Are you licensed and insured?
Nothing in print. Cody answers personally: “Cody handles that.”
Do you have reviews?
“We’re new to the area, so not many yet. Judge us on the work.”
What’s your per-acre rate?
The five drivers, then the lot walk.
Can you set my pad height?
“That’s your surveyor and the parish floodplain office. We build to what they give you.”
Do you handle the culvert and permits?
“The parish sizes the culvert and the application is yours to file. We’ll tell you what comes first.”
Can you just mulch it?
“Mulching is fine for trails, pasture and views. A house pad needs the stumps and roots out.”
Can you trim a tree by my house?
“That’s tree work, not dirt work, so we’re not the right crew.”

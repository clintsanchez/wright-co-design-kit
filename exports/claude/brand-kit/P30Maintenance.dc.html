Non-negotiables
  • Composite the supplied emblem files only; never redraw, retype or generate any part; never set “WRIGHT CO” in type as the logo.
  • The transparent emblem on Soil only; on Bone, the Soil square in a square Soil container. 240 px minimum, one-tenth clear space.
  • The favicon is interim; the five missing logo versions are labelled slots.
  • Zero photographs: every photo position is a “Real job photo:” slot. No stock, no AI scenes, no renders.
  • Dark-first soil palette only. One thing in rust. No green, yellow, blue, gradients or shadows.
  • Graduate 400 with font-synthesis none; Source Sans 3 for everything else.
  • Radius 2, 4 or 8 px, or a chamfer. No pills, no rounded buttons.
  • “Book a Free Lot Walk” and “Call or Text 360-957-5865”, equal width, primary first.
  • The sequence exactly; the offers always with their descriptors.
  • No price, credential, rating, review, address, hours, “LLC”, years-in-place claim, competitor phrasing, pad height or guarantee.
  • Every unconfirmed item keeps its Confirm before publishing badge.

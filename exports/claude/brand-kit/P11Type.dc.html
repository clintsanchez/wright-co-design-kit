Graduate ships in one weight. Load family=Graduate only; every Graduate element sets font-weight: 400 and font-synthesis: none. Uppercase, eight words and two lines at most. Never for H3, buttons, nav, labels or paragraphs. Never “WRIGHT CO” set in it as a logo.

Rules. Sentence case in source; CSS uppercases display and buttons. Left aligned; centre only a short hero line or closing CTA. No italics for emphasis. Body 18 px; nothing on the web below 14 px. Tabular figures for phones and tables. Without the brand fonts: Rockwell Bold headings (Arial Black fallback), Arial body. Avoid: Oswald or any condensed grotesk, grunge or script faces, light weights, a third family.

Social, 1080 × 1350: headline 72–96 px, support 40–44 px, label 28–30 px, numeral 160–240 px, smallest text 36 px, margins 80 px. Slides, 1920 × 1080: headings 64–80 px, body 28 px minimum, 30 words a slide. Print: headings 32–40 pt, body 10 pt minimum on paper, 18 pt on slides.

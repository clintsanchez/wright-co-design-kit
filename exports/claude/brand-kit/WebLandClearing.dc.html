Clearing for a house is different

Mulching is fine for trails, pasture and views. A house pad needs the stumps and roots out, so the ground under it can be built up and packed.

Forestry mulching or land clearing?

Stumps out

We pull stumps as part of clearing your lot. Grinding one stump in a yard isn’t our job; we do lot-scale work.

Lot-scale only

We clear lots for building. A single tree near a house is tree work, not dirt work, so we’re not the right crew for it.

Do you need a permit to clear?

Depending on how much you clear, the parish may need an application from you. We’ll tell you what applies to your lot; the application is yours to file.

Why we wait for firm ground

South Louisiana ground gets soft. When it’s too wet, we don’t run equipment. It ruts your lot and makes a worse pad.

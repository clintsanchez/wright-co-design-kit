Prohibited claims and unconfirmed facts

This section overrides everything else in this document.

The rule. A claim appears only if this kit gives it with its source. A fact that is not confirmed is a visible Confirm before publishing field that says what is needed. Never soften a prohibited claim into a near synonym, an icon, a badge, a number in a chart, an image, alt text or a hashtag. Several of these are live on the company’s own website and Facebook page today; that is not permission to repeat them.

Section 04 · Brand law

Iconography and devices

The numbered sequence is the one graphic device that is this company’s own, taken from the owner’s own description of the work.

The sequence strip
01 Clear · 02 Driveway · 03 Pad · 04 Foundation dig
Sequence cards
01ClearTrees and stumps out.Land clearing
02DrivewayCut in so trucks can get to your home site.Driveways & culverts
03PadYour house pad, built up to the height on your survey.House pads
04Foundation digDug the way your builder specifies, then handed off.House pads

Forms: a strip, four chamfered cards, a numbered list, one giant numeral on a social step card. Running text: “clear → driveway → pad”. Never re-ordered, renamed (“Drive”, “Step 1”) or mixed with ponds, drainage or hauling.

Rust rule
3–4 px Cattail Rust under an H2, at the top of the footer, as the before/after divider.
Stitched patch
The Homeplace Package
Offer badges, “Dirt Order #3”, the service-area tag. Never a shield or arch.
Icons

One filled set: Phosphor (Fill) or Tabler (filled), proposed; not yet chosen or bundled. Solid glyphs with square ends, or 2 px+ strokes with square caps. 24 px beside text, 20 in a button, 32 on cards, 48 max on a page. Clay or Copper on dark, Soil Black on Bone. An icon always has a text label; the phone icon never stands in for the number.

Clear space and minimum sizes, at true scale
480 pxHatched: 48 px clear space, one tenth of the width, on every side. Hero and covers. Everything reads: lettering, distressing, cattails, the phone digits.
320 px (needs 32 px clear): footer, closing slides. 240 px (needs 24 px clear): the minimum. Phone digits still read at 240.
200 and 160 px: not allowed. The phone digits blur, the cattails and grass turn to noise and the distressing reads as dirt on the lettering. Below 240 px use the wordmark or small mark once drawn; until then, no logo. Print: 2 in minimum; above about 4 in wide needs the vector master (W-27).

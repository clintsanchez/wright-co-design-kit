About this document

This is the working brand kit for Wright Co. It sets how the company looks, sounds and stays honest across the website, Facebook, a future Google Business Profile, print, signs and vehicles. The name, the emblem, the colours sampled from it, the phone, the email and the web address are the company’s own. Everything else is a BlakSheep Creative proposal waiting on the owner. Use it to build: every rule names its token, every specimen is drawn from the live system, and every fact still waiting on the owner carries a Confirm before publishing badge.

Read section 07 first if you are publishing today. It overrides everything else in this document.

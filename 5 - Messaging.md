## Core message

> **Wright Co. helps people around Hammond who've bought land to build a home get it build-ready, by sending one crew to clear it, cut in the driveway and build the house pad, in order, with no hand-offs.**

The anchor, not a tagline. Every headline, ad and estimate traces back to its four parts: **the finish line** (build-ready), **the sequence** (clear → driveway → pad), **one crew** (no hand-offs) and **the place** (the Hammond area).

## Primary positioning statement

> For people around Hammond who've bought raw land to build a home, Wright Co. is the home-site prep crew that takes your lot from woods to build-ready (cleared, driveway in, house pad built) with one crew, because we do the dirt side and only the dirt side, in order, with no hand-offs to subs who blame each other.

Internal. Not a headline. **In four words: build-ready, one crew, in order.**

The category is **home-site prep, raw land to build-ready**: not "excavation" (generic, commercial-sounding) and not "land clearing" (the most crowded and most price-shopped corner of the market). Ponds, drainage and hauling are add-ons to the home site, never headline categories.

## Primary customer problems

### Problem 1: "I bought the land. Who do I even call first?"

They've closed, the builder needs a pad, and site work isn't in the bid.

**Brand response:** Start with the order. In Tangipahoa it's your 911 address, then the culvert permit, then us: clear, driveway, pad. The Dirt Order walks it step by step. Book a free lot walk and we'll look at yours.

### Problem 2: "I don't want four outfits blaming each other."

**Brand response:** One crew clears it, cuts in the driveway and builds the pad, in that order. You make one call. If something on the lot isn't right, there's no question whose job it was.

### Problem 3: "What's it going to cost, and will there be surprises?"

**Brand response:** Price comes down to five things: acreage, how thick the trees are, stumps, how far the fill hauls, and how high the pad needs to be. We'll walk it and put it in writing. No figure, no range, no "from". "Firm price" and "written scope" are `CONFIRM BEFORE PUBLISHING`.

### Problem 4: "How high does my pad need to be? I don't want water in the house."

**Brand response:** Your surveyor and the parish set the height. We build to it, and we'll show you where to look it up. Never an elevation, never a flood promise.

### Problem 5: "What if it rains? What if they disappear?"

**Brand response:** South Louisiana ground gets soft. When it's too wet, we don't run equipment, because it ruts your lot and makes a worse pad. "We'll text you when we're rained out and when we're back" is `CONFIRM BEFORE PUBLISHING`. "Call or text Cody" is safe now.

## Objection handling

### "How long have you been doing this?"

Response:
"I've been clearing and excavating since 2022." Nothing about where. Never years in Louisiana or Hammond. Where he worked before is `CONFIRM BEFORE PUBLISHING`.

### "Are you licensed and insured?"

Response:
Cody answers personally with whatever is true at the time. Nothing in print. A crew member says: "Cody handles that. Let me have him call you."

### "Do you have reviews?"

Response:
"We're new to the area, so not many yet. Judge us on the work." Then the Free Lot Walk.

### "What does it cost? What's your per-acre rate?"

Response:
"It depends on five things: acreage, how thick the trees are, stumps, how far the fill hauls, and how high the pad needs to be. I'd rather walk it and give you a real number than guess." No figure, no range, no per-acre rate.

### "Can you set my pad height?"

Response:
"That's your surveyor and the parish floodplain office. We build to what they give you, and I'll show you where to look it up."

### "Do you handle the culvert and the permits?"

Response:
"The parish sizes the culvert and the application is yours to file. We'll tell you what comes first." Whether Wright Co. installs the culvert is `CONFIRM BEFORE PUBLISHING`.

### "Can you just mulch it? It's cheaper."

Response:
Mulching is fine for trails, pasture and views. A house pad needs the stumps and roots out. Fair to mulching; no competitor named.

### "Can you trim a tree by my house?"

Response:
"That's tree work, not dirt work, so we're not the right crew. If you ever need the lot cleared for a house, call me."

### "I'm a builder. Can you be my pad sub?" (builders)

Response:
"Text me the address and the survey. We'll build the pad to it." `CONFIRM BEFORE PUBLISHING`: "text us the survey", "one invoice", holding dates, in-house hauling.

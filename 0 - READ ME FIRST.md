## The nature of the job

This is a system built around an existing emblem. It is not a rebrand.

**What stays:** the name; the emblem, exactly as supplied; the colours sampled from it.

**What is proposed around them:** colour roles with two derived colours for contrast; two typefaces; a position, a tagline and three named offers; a voice; one graphic device taken from the owner's own description of the work (the numbered sequence); a square, heavy component set; and a set of templates.

**What is not proposed:** a redrawn emblem, a new name, a mascot, a monogram, a third typeface, or any colour outside `tokens/css-tokens.css`. The missing logo versions (vector master, no-phone emblem, flat wordmark, small mark, one-colour versions) are briefed for a human designer in `assets/logos/README.md` and appear in layouts only as labelled slots.

The idea to design from: the page should look like the ground itself, and like a local crew's truck door: dark soil, rust and bone, heavy collegiate lettering, square edges, one rust button.

## Eight things to know before designing anything

1. **The emblem is composited, never drawn.** It is a detailed raster illustration. It works only at 240 px wide or more, and only on Soil grounds; on Bone or white it sits inside a square-cornered Soil container (use `wright-co-emblem-on-soil-square-*.png`). The phone number is baked into it and is confirmed.
2. **Zero photographs.** Every photo position is a labelled slot that reads "Real job photo: [what]". No stock, no AI scenes, no illustrated lots, no equipment renders, ever.
3. **Dark-first.** Soil Black carries the page; Bone carries long reading; Cattail Rust marks the one thing to press.
4. **Graduate at weight 400 only, with `font-synthesis: none`.** Source Sans 3 for everything else. Radius 2 / 4 / 8 px. No pills, no rounded buttons, no gradients, no shadows.
5. **Two buttons, exactly these words, equal width:** "Book a Free Lot Walk" (solid) and "Call or Text 360-957-5865" (outlined). The phone is a button, never a bare link.
6. **The sequence, exactly:** 01 Clear · 02 Driveway · 03 Pad · 04 Foundation Dig. Same words, same order, everywhere.
7. **No price, credential, rating, address, "LLC" or years-in-place claim.** "Clearing and excavating since 2022" is the only history line.
8. **The brand describes only itself.** No competitor named or described, none of their phrasing ("standing pine", "slab", "every phase", "one contractor", a bare "start to finish"), and nothing from the owner's separate business in another state.

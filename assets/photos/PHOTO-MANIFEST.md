## Rules for the whole set

1. **Real Wright Co Louisiana jobs only.** Every photo is of work Wright Co did, in Louisiana, shot by Cody, the crew or a customer with permission.
2. **No stock, no AI images presented as jobs.** Never use a stock photo, an AI-generated image or another company's photo to stand in for Wright Co work, in any layout, mock-up or draft that could be published. Clearly illustrative graphics (an icon-based before → after sequence diagram) are fine and are labelled as graphics, not photos.
3. **No Washington business photos.** Nothing from any other business the owner has run, or from out of state, is used as Wright Co work. (The phone is a 360 number; that does not make Washington photos relevant.)
4. **Caption every job photo with the town and the work** (for example "Lot clearing and house pad, [town], LA"). Never a client's name without permission and never an invented number (acres, yards, days, price).
5. **Crop and straighten only.** One warm, slightly desaturated grade on everything (greens pulled down 15–20%, shadows lifted a touch, red clay and soil kept true), per `brand/identity.md` §05. No compositing, object removal, generative fill or expansion, no upscaling, no heavy HDR.
6. **Another company's equipment, logo or truck lettering is never the subject.** Crop it out or do not use the frame.
7. **Faces, house numbers and plates** of customers and neighbours are cropped out unless cleared.
8. **Type on a photo** sits on a 60–75% Soil Black `#120C06` overlay.

## Labelled photo slots (required until real photos exist)

Every layout (web, social, print, deck) that would hold a job photo uses a **labelled slot** instead: a Soil `#20150C` or `#2E1D10` box at the final crop ratio, a thin Clay border, and a label in Source Sans 3 naming the shot it needs, for example:

> PHOTO SLOT: before / during / after, same lot, same spot (4:5) — Wright Co job, Louisiana

The slot stays visibly a slot. It is never filled with stock, AI imagery or a blurred placeholder photo. Where a hero would hold a photo, a type-led hero on Soil with the emblem is the approved alternative (`brand/identity.md` §05).

## Shot list to request from Cody

From `brand/identity.md` §05. Phone photos are fine; send originals (full size, not screenshots or Facebook downloads).

1. **The hero series: before → during → after of ONE lot, shot from the same spot.** Mark the spot (a stake or a fence post) and shoot the same frame at each stage: woods / cleared and stumped / driveway cut in / pad built and compacted. Landscape and vertical at each stage.
2. **Driveway cut**, with the culvert set at the road if there is one.
3. **The finished house pad**, compacted, ideally with the builder's stakes in.
4. **A pond** holding water (and, if possible, being dug).
5. **Equipment working**: mulcher in brush, a stump coming out, gravel being dumped, a pad being rolled. Wright Co's own machines, no other company's lettering in frame.
6. **Crew in gear, actually working**, and Cody walking a lot with a landowner (with their permission).
7. **Short vertical video (10–30 s)**: mulcher through brush, stump grinding, a pad being rolled, a time-lapse from the fixed spot.

For each photo, record: the town (parish is fine), the date, what work it shows, who shot it, and whether any customer or property in it has agreed to its use.

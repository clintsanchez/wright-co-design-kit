## Where this client departs from the universal prompt

- No photograph exists, so every photo position is a `PhotoSlot` whose label begins "Real job photo:", and no template that depends on one may be posted. No stock, no AI scenes, no equipment renders.
- The emblem is a detailed raster with the phone in its banner. It is used only at 480 px or more on a 1080 px canvas, on dark grounds or inside a Soil container on Bone, so most feed cards carry no logo.
- No button is drawn on a social graphic (file 8); the two CTAs are a line of text.
- Families added for this client: step cards 01 to 04, The Dirt Order tips, the Free Lot Walk, the seasonal "Get your lot ready this winter. Build in spring.", now booking and service area, the Dirt Order teaser, operations notices.
- Families reduced to empty or badged templates: review (one future template, bannered, no sample text), before and after (slots), job photo (slot), now booking (badged headline), announcement (badged fields).
- Families dropped: quote, testimonial content, case study, client results, reports, offers and promotions with prices, problem-agitation-solution, comparison, myth versus fact, hiring, community, event, podcast, webinar, thought leadership, commentary, memes, storm cleanup, meet the crew, equipment showcase.

## Decisions made in file 7 that the shared brief did not state

- The emblem is placed at 480 px or wider on a 1080 px canvas (file 8 sets 240 px as the floor; feeds are seen at about a third of size). It appears once per canvas.
- No corner mark is built; the drawn small mark (logo #4) does not exist and is not approximated.
- Decoration is three gestures (giant numeral, rust rule, contour lines), one per canvas. Soil grain is ground texture, not a gesture. The stitched patch is a label component.
- The step-card numeral is Copper Graduate at 200 to 240 px; the step label stays "03 Pad" with the one-line copy carrying "house pad".
- Social padding is 80 px on feed canvases and 64 px on shallow landscapes and banners.
- The Google Business Profile post family is built now, labelled "to be created", with type-led variants until real photos exist.
- Instagram uses the shared masters only, labelled "no account on file" (file 8).
- The seasonal family uses the offer plan's honest version only. "Driest months" and "booking winter lots now" are badged and off by default. No price, discount, bonus, guarantee or settling claim.
- Hashtags: three at most on Facebook from a proposed list of six; none on Google; none in artwork.
- The Facebook page cover uses 1640 x 924, not the universal 851 x 315; the row is flagged.
- Avatar previews at 256, 128, 64 and 32 px are downscales of the supplied files with a written legibility report, not new files.
- The review component exists only as an empty future template with a permanent banner and no sample text, not even a fake one.
- Platform sizes are working values from the universal prompt and earlier kits; none was checked against a platform on 2026-10-15.

**Success.** The system reads as one design system producing many formats, and every graphic unmistakably belongs to this crew: dark soil, a heavy bone headline, one copper numeral or rust rule, the sequence in the same words every time, and a plain sentence that is still true when a landowner checks it.
